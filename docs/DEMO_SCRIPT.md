# CEO/CFO demonstration script

Target length: 10–15 minutes. This is a planned walkthrough, not a record of a completed demo. All internal messages, policies, amounts, and parties are fictional.

## 1. Introduce the problem — one minute

"Important decisions often arrive as an email with several attachments. The work is finding the conditions, comparing documents, and making sure nothing is missed. This demonstration helps prepare that review while keeping the decision with your team."

Connect briefly to the earlier website and SEO proposals: those improve how people find and interact with ACA; this proposal explores the work that follows inside the organization. Do not claim any measured benefit from the earlier proposals.

## 2. Open an executive review message — two minutes

Open the fictional development colleague's forwarded restricted-gift offer. Identify sender, request, attachments, and why CEO/CFO review may be needed. Show the current mode: local example, live model, and/or connected test inbox.

Run identification. Show the proposed owner, request category, deadlines explicitly found in the documents, and any review flags. Do not treat a classification as permission to accept terms or reply automatically.

## 3. Inspect the attachments — two minutes

Open the original gift letter beside its extracted summary. Click an amount or deadline to see the supporting page. Explain the restrictions and identify missing details without giving a final legal or accounting conclusion.

For the OCR example, switch to a scanned invoice. Show that image text is actually extracted; then open an unclear sample and demonstrate that an unreadable field remains unknown. An expected transcript is an evaluation aid, not an OCR result.

## 4. Ask a follow-up — three minutes

Ask: "What should we clarify before accepting this offer, and what supports that recommendation?"

The activity view should reveal actual searches and document reads as they happen. Explain: "It checks the documents it has permission to use. If it needs more evidence, it searches again."

Inspect the final answer's citations. Open the gift terms and the sample review policy from the document list. Show page/section references and explicitly distinguish the sample policy from actual ACA policy. Display the short action explanations as explanations, not as a complete record of private model thought.

Ask an unsupported question such as "Has the donor already transferred the money?" Without a payment record, the answer must say it cannot establish that. An offer letter is insufficient evidence.

## 5. Prepare a response — one minute

Create a proposed reply acknowledging the offer and requesting clarification. Show the staff review step. Use a local draft/outbox demonstration; do not send an email or imply a gift was accepted.

Explain that a later low-risk receipt acknowledgment could be automatic under explicit rules. Acceptance of conditions and financial actions remain outside that category.

## 6. Open the Langfuse view — two minutes

Follow the workflow's actual trace into Langfuse when connected. Show operations, sources as permitted metadata, timing, model usage, review outcome, and estimated cost if available. Keep raw technical details in a presenter view.

If Langfuse is disconnected, say so and show the local activity record. Never imply a successful cloud export. Explain that raw correspondence need not be included in the cloud trace.

## 7. Discuss privacy and pilot economics — two minutes

Show where source documents, retrieved passages, and activity records go. Explain the available deployment choices and the need to verify provider retention terms. Do not promise all processing stays with ACA when using an external model.

For the CFO, distinguish measured run time and usage from projected savings. A pilot should measure current handling time, AI-assisted review time, correction frequency, staff adoption, and total operating cost. Staff capacity released is not automatically a cash saving or a staffing reduction.

Illustrative calculation only: monthly time released = completed tasks × (baseline minutes − assisted minutes including review) / 60. Multiply by a loaded hourly cost only when estimating capacity value; include implementation, hosting, search, model, monitoring, and maintenance costs separately. Measure completed tasks and exceptions rather than counting drafts as work finished.

## Optional extension

Prepare a short board-finance briefing from a synthetic budget-versus-actual packet. Show the source cells for each calculation. Add this after the primary workflow is reliable; it should not delay the first complete demonstration.
